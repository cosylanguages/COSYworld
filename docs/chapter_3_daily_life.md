# Chapter 3: Daily Life

## Overview
Chapter 3 introduces the player to daily routines and time structures in COSY Town under a Monolingual Learning Architecture without bilingual translations or cards. The player completes a morning routine across apartment rooms, follows a daily schedule out onto the Street and Bus Stop, and masters adverbs of frequency.

---

## Scenes & Room Topology (5 Scenes)

1. **Bedroom** (`apartment_bedroom`)
   - District: Residential District
   - Features: Bed, desk, alarm clock (`alarm_clock`).
   - Routine context: Waking up, studying, checking time.

2. **Bathroom** (`apartment_bathroom`)
   - District: Residential District
   - Features: Towel, soap, vanity mirror (`bathroom_mirror`).
   - Routine context: Brushing teeth, washing face.

3. **Kitchen** (`apartment_kitchen`)
   - District: Residential District
   - Features: Refrigerator, kettle, cups.
   - Routine context: Cooking, eating breakfast, drinking tea/coffee.

4. **Street** (`street`)
   - District: City Centre
   - Features: City Avenue buildings, street clock (`street_clock`), street lamp.
   - Routine context: Commuting to work/study.

5. **Bus Stop** (`bus_stop`)
   - District: City Centre
   - Features: Bus shelter, bus stop sign (`bus_stop_sign`), timetable board (`bus_timetable_board`).
   - Routine context: Waiting for the bus, tracking transport schedules.

---

## Vocabulary Categories

### 1. Routine Verbs & Actions
- `wake up`, `brush`, `wash`, `eat`, `drink`, `cook`, `work`, `study`, `sleep`

### 2. Time & Objects
- `clock`, `morning`, `afternoon`, `evening`, `bus_stop_sign`, `bus_timetable_board`

### 3. Adverbs of Frequency
- `always`, `usually`, `often`, `sometimes`, `never`

---

## Grammar Concepts & Patterns

1. **`Present Simple`** (`gt_present_simple`): Describing habitual actions (`I wake up at seven o'clock`).
2. **`Time`** (`gt_time`): Telling time and periods of day (`It is eight o'clock in the morning`).
3. **`Frequency`** (`gt_frequency`): Expressing habit frequency using adverbs (`I always brush my teeth`).

---

## Communication Goals

- **Describe your day**: Express daily routines using Present Simple (`I wake up, brush my teeth, and eat breakfast`).
- **Ask the time**: Inquire about current time (`What time is it?`).
- **Talk about habits**: State how often actions occur (`I usually take the bus`).

---

## Gameplay Quest Progression (3 Steps)

1. **Complete an Entire Morning Routine**: Check the alarm clock in the bedroom, brush teeth, wash, and eat breakfast.
2. **Follow a Daily Schedule**: Walk out to the street and check the street clock to track daily schedule.
3. **Unlock Routine Achievements**: Travel to the bus stop and check the bus schedule to complete Chapter 3.

---

## Modular Achievements

- `ach_morning_master`: Early Bird (Complete full morning routine).
- `ach_schedule_tracker`: Schedule Tracker (Follow a daily schedule across town).
- `ach_routine_habit_master`: Habit Master (Master routine verbs, time, and adverbs of frequency).
