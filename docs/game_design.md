# COSY World Game Design Manifesto

## Philosophy: "Learn by Living, Not by Translating"

COSY World is designed around **Inductive Direct Immersion**. Language learning in traditional apps relies heavily on paired L1-L2 translations, flashcards, and grammar drills. COSY World replaces translation with natural contextual experience:

1. **Visual Cause-and-Effect Sequences**:
   - Instead of translating "Key = Clé", the player sees a key (`🔑`), uses it on a closed door (`🚪`), and unlocks the path (`🔓 Open`). Language is anchored directly to real-world action.
2. **Contextual NPC Interactions**:
   - Town NPCs communicate with natural visual gestures (waving 👋, pointing 👉, modeling speech 🗣️) and simple, immersive dialogue choices.
3. **Peaceful Open World**:
   - COSY Town is a stress-free environment without time penalties or failing conditions. Exploration rewards the player with Citizen XP, new town districts, and grammar tree unlocks.
4. **Multilingual Inclusivity**:
   - Supports 14 target languages (English, French, Italian, Spanish, German, Russian, Greek, Portuguese, Armenian, Georgian, Breton, Tatar, Bashkir, Chuvash) with identical educational parity.
