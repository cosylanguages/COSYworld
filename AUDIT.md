## Critical issues (must fix before launch)

- [ ] Horizontal overflow / clipped content on small screens

- [ ] Feedback only via color (no text/icon/pattern)

## High priority
- [ ] Long place names or translations break layout
- [ ] Discovery, filtering, or search feedback unclear
- [ ] Inconsistent component behavior across pages

- [ ] Cultural context is missing, unclear, or represented by stereotypes
- [ ] Reading layout is difficult on mobile or at large text sizes

## Medium / Low
- [ ] Visual hierarchy unclear

- [ ] Overuse of decorative effects (gradients, glass, etc.)
- [ ] Inconsistent spacing / typography
- [ ] Content collections are difficult to browse or resume
